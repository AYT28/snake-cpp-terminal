Welcome! You can start by choosing a color for your snake:
1. Green
2. White
3. Purple
4. Yellow
Press 1-4 to choose color

Mechanics:
There are safe foods(which are red) and unsafe foods(which are blue). 
If you eat safe foods, you gain 10 score.
But if you eat unsafe food, you lose 5 score.
Also unsafe food disappears after 5 seconds when they spawn. 
Lose conditions are:
1. Hitting walls
2. Your head hitting yourself
3. Falling under 0 score

Have Fun :)
